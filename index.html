About Me

Hello, I’m Nhial Gatkuoth Rut. I’m 25 years old and currently studying Electrical and Computer Engineering at Wollega University. My Journey: From Akobo to the Future of South Sudan I was born in a small, resilient village in South Sudan known as Akobo County. My childhood was rooted in the strength of my community, but like many of my peers, my life was upended when conflict broke out. The war forced me to leave my home and seek refuge in Ethiopia, a transition that marked the beginning of a challenging yet transformative chapter in my life. Upon arriving in Ethiopia, I remained steadfast in my commitment to education. I resumed my studies in Grade 8, navigating a new curriculum and environment. Through hard work and perseverance, I successfully sat for the Ethiopian National Entrance Examination. My academic performance earned me a placement at Wollega University, where I began my journey as a freshman. Driven by a passion for technology and problem-solving, I excelled in my freshman courses and qualified for the Pre-Engineering track. When the time came to specialize, I chose Electrical and Computer Engineering as my first choice. Today, I am proud to be a third-year student in this rigorous field. My goal is to complete my Bachelor’s degree at Wollega University with excellence. However, my journey does not end with a diploma. My ultimate ambition is to return to South Sudan and use the technical expertise I have gained in Ethiopia to serve my community. Whether through developing infrastructure, improving telecommunications, or mentoring the next generation of students, I am dedicated to contributing to the rebuilding and progress of South Sudan as a whole. My passion lies in computer programming, where I enjoy solving problems and building creative solutions.
