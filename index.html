About

Engineering student from Wollega University, passionate about programming and electronics. # About Me ## My Journey: From Akobo to the Future of South Sudan Hello! I'm **Nhial Gatkuoth Rut**, a third-year **Electrical and Computer Engineering** student at **Wollega University** in Ethiopia. I am passionate about technology, innovation, and using engineering to create solutions that improve people's lives. I was born in **Akobo County**, a resilient community in South Sudan. My childhood was shaped by the strength and determination of my people. However, like many others, my life changed dramatically when conflict forced my family to leave our home and seek refuge in Ethiopia. Although this transition brought many challenges, it also opened the door to new opportunities and strengthened my determination to pursue education. After arriving in Ethiopia, I resumed my studies in **Grade 8**, adapting to a new educational system and environment. Through dedication, perseverance, and hard work, I successfully completed the Ethiopian National Entrance Examination, earning admission to **Wollega University**. My passion for technology and problem-solving inspired me to pursue engineering. After excelling in my freshman studies and qualifying for the Pre-Engineering program, I chose **Electrical and Computer Engineering** as my field of specialization. Today, I am proud to continue my academic journey as a third-year student, developing the knowledge and practical skills needed to contribute to the rapidly evolving world of engineering and technology. My vision extends beyond earning a bachelor's degree. I aspire to use my education and technical expertise to support the development of **South Sudan**. I hope to contribute to infrastructure development, modern telecommunications, digital innovation, and technology education while mentoring young people who dream of building a brighter future for our nation. I believe that education is a powerful tool for transformation. Every challenge I have faced has strengthened my determination to make a meaningful difference. My journey from Akobo to Wollega University represents not only personal growth but also my commitment to serving my community and helping shape a stronger, more prosperous South Sudan. Thank you for visiting my website. I invite you to explore my projects, learn more about my work, and connect with me as I continue this journey of learning, innovation, and service.
